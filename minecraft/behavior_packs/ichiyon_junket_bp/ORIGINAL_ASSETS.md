# JUNKET original production assets

The venues, textures and display models in this behavior pack are generated by
`scripts/minecraft/build_junket_original_world.py`. They are independently
authored and do not contain the licensed map's resource-pack artwork, models,
sounds, datapacks or world data.

The original `JUNKET CRAFT ver1.09` archive remains local-only. Its terms forbid
secondary distribution, so no source-derived file belongs in Git, a managed
release archive or the production Minecraft server.
